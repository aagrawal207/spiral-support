A native iPhone game

Find your
way down.

Turn the tower beneath a bouncing ball. The ball falls as soon as a run starts and bounces off safe platforms on its own; drag left or right at any time, even mid-air, to line up the next gap. Play Classic or hazard-free Zen.

Learn to steer with one safe practice drop. The game pauses after your first gap so you can finish the short tutorial, then tap "Start game" when you are ready. During play, small marks on the upcoming rings predict where you land if you stop turning. Pause whenever you want the full explanation.

Gold restores bounce energy. A blue Shield protects one hazardous landing. Score one point per gap, with one extra for a Perfect. There is no multiplier or score limit.

Choose a mode

Two ways to play.

01 / Classic

Chase your best.

Classic starts with five energy segments. Bright pieces are full; dark, outlined slots are empty. Each ring gives two free bounces; later bounces cost energy, and gold restores it. Unprotected hazards or empty energy end the run. Three gaps in a row charge a two-point smash. Every twelve cleared rings adds five points and brings a harder tower.

See the scoring rules →

02 / Zen

Play without hazards.

Keep the same bouncing and steering, with no hazards or energy loss. Follow the openings at your own pace and finish a run when you choose. Gold and three-gap smashes remain available. Zen keeps its own best score and depth, separate from Classic.

Controls and color

Adjust the feel.

Set your sensitivity, reverse the drag direction, or reduce decorative motion. Gameplay movement, including drifting rings and the guide following them, remains active. Sound and haptics have separate switches.

A clear top display keeps score, energy, Shield, and Pause easy to find. Detailed route text and statistics are on the paused screen.

Choose one of three palettes:

  • Lagoon
  • Clay
  • Midnight

Personal bests and recent runs are stored on your device, subject to your backup settings. Read the privacy policy.
